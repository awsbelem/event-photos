# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Attendees** of AWS Community Day Brasil — Edição Norte (31 Oct 2026, FACI Wyden, Belém/PA). On their phones, during the event or in the days after, often on mobile data. Job: find themselves in the photos (by scrolling the schedule or with a selfie), save a photo, and post it with an event frame tagging @awscommunitydaynorte.
- **Organizers, sponsors and the AWS community.** The gallery is also the event's public showcase: proof of what the day was, shared beyond the people who attended.
- **Photographers** (volunteers, each with a personal code) upload from their phones through the upload page, usually at the venue.

## Product Purpose

Deliver the event's photos to the people in them within hours, already developed and framed in the event's brand, so posting about the event is one tap. Success: attendees find their photos, post them framed and tagged, and the gallery reads as a worthy record of the CD Norte.

## Positioning

Built for this event: photos are culled, developed and framed automatically, grouped by the moments of the event's own schedule, credited to each photographer, and findable by selfie, delivered as a light static site rather than a generic photo-sharing album.

## Operating Context

- Gallery: static site on S3 + CloudFront, generated by `src/event_photos/gallery.py`; sections follow the event schedule in `event.json`; full-screen viewer with frame choice (feed 4:5 / story 9:16), save or share, copy the @handle; optional selfie search (Amazon Rekognition) with explicit consent.
- Upload page: `src/event_photos/inbox_app.py`, served from a Lambda URL; photographer enters their code, picks photos, sees per-file progress.
- Rehearsal events (`cd-norte-2026-treino`, `cd-norte-2026-jantar`) use the same pages.
- Operator review (`review.py`) and the POC report (`report.py`) are internal tools, not product surfaces.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Deadline: the event is Saturday, 31 Oct 2026; gallery and upload page must be ready before it.
- Static, no server and no database for the gallery; pages are single HTML files with inline CSS/JS generated from Python.
- Must stay fast on mobile data with thousands of photos (tiles built client-side from a JSON list).
- Interface copy is Portuguese (pt-BR); code is English.
- Gallery is `noindex`, not framed by other sites.

## Brand Commitments

- The product is designed for AWS Community Day Norte specifically, not as a white-label per event. A future event would get its own redesign.
- The web pages (gallery, upload page) follow the official event artwork in `assets/frames/aws-community-day-belem/` (navy agenda slide, hexagon cubes, Belém skyline). Its typeface is unconfirmed; Inter stands in until the organizers send it.
- Post-studio brand kit (plum): `assets/brand/cd-norte/` (`brand.json`, logo, hexagons, Norte region map, tacacá illustration, IBM Plex Sans and Permanent Marker fonts). Its rules ("orange to call, white to read", one gradient per artwork, hexagons in opposite corners, map only as texture and never behind photos) are binding for the photo frames, which stay plum by decision. On the web pages, "orange to call, white to read" holds.
- Handle @awscommunitydaynorte; site awscommunityday.com.br/norte.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real schedule for the day (`events/aws-community-day-belem-2026/event.json`): keynotes by Vanessa Santos and Marcelo Paiva, opening, breaks, closing.
- Three frame designs (`assets/frames/cd-norte-*`) and the header/footer artwork for AWS Community Day Belém.
- No event photos exist yet (the event hasn't happened); demonstrations use the rehearsal run's Wikimedia Commons photos (credited in its CREDITS.json) and must be labeled as such. Their booths are other events' sponsors, not CD Norte's.
- No sponsor list or sponsor logos in the repository; do not invent sponsors.

## Product Principles

1. The attendee's photo is the hero; the event's identity frames it, never competes with it.
2. One tap from seeing a photo to posting it framed and tagged.
3. Find yourself fast: schedule moments and selfie search beat endless scrolling.
4. Light enough for event Wi-Fi and 4G.
5. Credit the people who made the photos.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets of at least 44px, full keyboard use of the viewer, reduced-motion respected, explicit consent before any face comparison.
